Rabus Hair Africa Braiding

Rabus Hair Africa Braiding is a professional African hair braiding studio in Chicago, Illinois — known for high-quality, stylish, long-lasting braids. Our stylists specialize in Knotless Braids, Boho Knotless, Box Braids, Feed-In Braids, Two-Strand Twists, and Crochet installations, all tailored to your unique look.

We are committed to a clean, calm, and premium experience using top-quality braiding hair — beautiful results that protect your natural hair. Appointments are required so every client receives our full attention and top-tier service. Book your session today and leave our chair as a masterpiece.
