The Rabus Hair Promise

The following terms apply when booking your appointment with RABUS HAIR AFRICA BRAIDING. We reserve the exclusive right to decline service to any client at any time. By booking, you agree to the policies below.

Payment

All payments are made in person on the day of your appointment. No online payment and no deposit is required to book. Cash is accepted.

Pricing is subject to change at any time. Any change will not affect an appointment that has already been booked.

Appointments & Walk-Ins

Appointments are recommended. Walk-ins are welcome, but please call ahead at +1 (312) 647-0604 to confirm availability before coming to the salon.

Coming to Your Appointment

Please arrive with your hair freshly washed, blow-dried straight, and oil-free.

If your hair is not washed and blown out, a $30 wash & blow-out service will be added. If your hair is washed but not blown out, a $20 straightening service will be added.

If your hair is short, please send a photo via Instagram @rabus_hair or text message prior to booking.

Please arrive on time. A 15-minute grace period is extended to both the stylist and the client.

Please note: we do not offer braiding services for men — children are welcome.

We offer touch-up services — please call us to discuss your specific needs.

Hair Extensions

Details regarding hair extensions will be shared via text message once your appointment is confirmed.

Your braiding hair is included in the price of your style — there is nothing extra to buy.

You are welcome to bring your own hair if you prefer — this is optional, and especially helpful if we do not carry the colour or type you want.

Please share your desired colour at least 24 hours before your appointment so we can prepare it in advance.

If you are sensitive or allergic to hair extensions, we highly recommend adding an ACV (apple cider vinegar) rinse to your service for an additional $20.

An additional $20 applies to any custom-made color (a blend of multiple colors).

Cancellations & Rescheduling

A minimum of 24 hours' notice is required to reschedule your appointment. This applies to both new and returning clients — NO EXCEPTIONS.

Appointments may be rescheduled once within 30 days of the original booking, subject to availability.

Late cancellations and no-shows may be required to rebook, and repeated no-shows may not be offered future appointments.

Extras

Each time you refer a new client to us, you will receive a $25 discount on your next service.

Please plan your day accordingly — there is no need to rush the stylist, as it will not shorten the time required to complete your service.

During Your Service

If you have any questions or concerns, please let us know during your service rather than waiting until it is complete.

Response Time

We truly appreciate your business. Please allow up to 2 hours for a response during business hours. We are open every day, including Sundays, 7 AM – 6 PM.

— End of Terms & Conditions —
